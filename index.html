Privacy Policy for DailyFlow

Last Updated: November 5, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: DailyFlow is 100% offline-first. Your tasks, notes, habits, workouts, and focus sessions are stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, track, or share your personal information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

DailyFlow operates without accounts or remote tracking servers:

  • No Account Needed: No registration, login, or personal profile is required.
  • No Identity Collection: We do not ask for or collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: No usage tracking, behavioral telemetry, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising IDs: DailyFlow contains no ads and collects no advertising identifiers.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Your data is never uploaded to remote servers without your explicit manual export.

2. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All information created in the application resides in local device storage:

  • Tasks & Checklists: Items, priorities, due dates, and reminders.
  • Habits: Daily routine trackers and streak records.
  • Notes: Markdown notes and scratchpads.
  • Workouts: Exercise logs, routine templates, and calorie statistics.
  • Focus Sessions: Deep work and Pomodoro timer logs.

3. Device Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to alert you of scheduled task reminders.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to deliver timely reminders precisely when scheduled.
  • Storage / File System: Used only when you choose to manually export or import a JSON backup file.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data. To permanently delete all app data:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Apps > DailyFlow > Storage > Clear Data, or
  • Uninstall the DailyFlow application from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyFlow does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13, adhering to COPPA and GDPR regulations.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out to us at support@dailyflow.app.
